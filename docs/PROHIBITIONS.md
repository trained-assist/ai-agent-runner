# Prohibitions and restrictions

The most dangerous requirements are prohibitions and restrictions on rights. Before
prohibiting, disabling, or limiting something anywhere, collect and show evidence:

- what is being protected;
- which observations, tests, or incidents support it;
- which alternatives were considered;
- what is lost by the restriction.
